# Introduction plan

Publish a 30-second deployment example where PORT=70000 fails before startup. Share once in a backend community that welcomes tooling. Ask two maintainers to try a contract with their sanitized example.env; turn their parsing friction into regression tests.

Release one useful example first, then ask for workflow feedback where self-promotion is
allowed. Do not mass-post, message strangers, buy stars or imply endorsements.
Use a single canonical demo link and disclose that you maintain the project.

For the first seven days, record real issue reports, successful first runs and useful
contributions manually. Stars and profile follows are secondary observations, not success
guarantees. At day 7, improve the largest onboarding problem before widening promotion.
No benchmark or adoption claim should be published without a reproducible measurement.
